Is WorkNest free for job seekers?+

Yes. Creating a profile and applying to roles costs you nothing — companies pay us to run their hiring.

How is this different from a normal job board?+

A job board passes your application along and stops there. We read it, score it against the role, shortlist the strongest and keep you updated the whole way.

What happens after I apply?+

Your status moves from submitted to reviewed, then shortlisted or not — and you hear from us either way.

Who actually reviews my application?+

A member of the WorkNest talent team who works on that role directly — not an automated filter.

Which companies hire through WorkNest?+

Companies that bring us live, open roles. You'll see who's hiring on every listing.

How will I hear about updates?+

By email and in your applications dashboard, every time your status changes.
